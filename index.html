Player profile manager

Upload player portraits directly from this page—no need to add photos to GitHub.

Player images use Cloudinary’s free image hosting; Firebase Storage and a billing upgrade are not required. Configure the Cloudinary cloud name and restricted unsigned upload preset in this file first. Unsigned upload presets are public, so restrict formats and destination in Cloudinary.

Team & captain manager

Rename an existing team or add a new team, set its captain, and upload a team logo.

Team logos are uploaded to Cloudinary. Renaming a team updates its roster, fixtures, stats and auction team references. Adding a team does not automatically generate new fixtures.

Fixture generator & editor

Set how many league-stage matches each team should play. You can generate a randomized schedule after the auction or whenever you intentionally want a fresh schedule. Existing Week 1 and Rivalry Week fixtures stay unchanged unless you generate a replacement schedule.

Finals are generated automatically when all league-stage fixtures have results. You can edit home/away teams for each fixture in the Match Centre while signed in as admin.

Match poster studio

Generate a shareable result poster after entering the score and scorer details.

The poster includes the score, teams, scorer notes and POTM when selected. Download or share the generated graphic.
